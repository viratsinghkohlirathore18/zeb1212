# Zesh Brand Boutique

# Build a Premium Corporate Website for ZESH

Create a complete, premium, modern corporate website for **ZESH**, a women's bottom-wear fashion brand. The website must combine corporate branding, product portfolio presentation, and lead generation for retail customers, retailers, distributors, wholesalers, and bulk buyers.

## 1. IMPORTANT BUSINESS INFORMATION

Use the existing website as the primary source of business information:

**Existing website:** https://myzesh.in/

Before building the website, review the existing website and extract all available and relevant information, including:

- Brand name and brand story
- Company introduction and business details
- Product categories and product descriptions
- Existing brand identity and logo
- Product photographs and available visual assets
- Contact information and business address
- Existing brand messaging and policies

Reuse verified information from the existing website. Do not invent company history, certifications, manufacturing facilities, customer testimonials, sales figures, or other business claims.

If any information is unavailable, omit it or leave it editable for future updates. Do not copy unrelated demo content, template information, or placeholder contact details.

## 2. WEBSITE DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a premium fashion-corporate design with a distinctive visual identity for ZESH.

Design requirements:

- Sophisticated, minimal, elegant, and professional appearance.
- Premium fashion-editorial photography.
- Refined typography and excellent visual hierarchy.
- Generous whitespace and balanced layouts.
- A coordinated colour palette inspired by the existing ZESH branding.
- Subtle transitions and polished hover effects.
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Consistent styling across every page.
- High-quality product imagery and carefully designed image galleries.

Avoid generic templates, excessive gradients, random colour combinations, unnecessary animations, overcrowded layouts, and repetitive card designs.

The website must look like a real, established fashion brand rather than a basic AI-generated website.

## 3. WEBSITE NAVIGATION

Create the following five main pages:

1. Home
2. About Us
3. Collections / Products
4. Portfolio / Gallery
5. Contact Us

Add two clear header actions:

- **Shop Online:** Link to https://myzesh.in/
- **Contact Us:** Open the corporate contact and enquiry page.

Keep the navigation clean, consistent, and mobile-friendly.

## 4. HOME PAGE

Build a visually impressive homepage with these sections in this exact order:

### A. Premium Hero Section
- Use a large, high-quality fashion image featuring women's bottom wear.
- Display the ZESH brand name and a concise, premium brand headline.
- Add a short introduction based on verified existing brand information.
- Include two prominent buttons: “Explore Collections” and “Business Enquiry”.
- Make the hero responsive and visually impactful.

### B. Brand Introduction
- Introduce ZESH using information extracted from the existing website.
- Communicate the brand's product focus and positioning.
- Add a button linking to About Us.

### C. Featured Collections
Show relevant product categories based on the actual catalogue, including:
- Pants
- Joggers
- Trousers
- Palazzos
- Leggings
- Track Pants

Use genuine product photographs wherever available. Each category must have a suitable image, title, and functional navigation to its corresponding collection or gallery.

Only display categories and products supported by actual business information.

### D. Brand Highlights
Present verified product features, brand values, or other available strengths. Do not invent quality certifications or unsupported claims.

### E. Product Portfolio Preview
Create an elegant fashion gallery using the actual product photographs and brand assets. Include a button linking to the full Portfolio / Gallery page.

### F. About ZESH Preview
Present a concise company introduction with an appropriate visual and a button linking to About Us.

### G. Business Enquiry Section
Create a premium call-to-action for retailers, distributors, wholesalers, resellers, and bulk buyers.

Use a strong headline such as “Let's Build a Business Partnership” and a button linking to Contact Us.

### H. Online Store Promotion
Clearly invite customers to explore and purchase products from the existing online store at https://myzesh.in/.

### I. Contact Preview
Display verified contact information and convenient enquiry options.

### J. Footer
Include:
- ZESH logo
- Short brand introduction
- Links to all five pages
- Shop Online link
- Verified business contact information
- Privacy Policy and applicable legal links
- Social media links only when verified URLs are available

## 5. ABOUT US PAGE

Create a dedicated About Us page containing:

- Premium introductory banner
- Company and brand overview
- Brand story based on available website information
- Mission and vision, only where supported by existing information
- Brand philosophy and product focus
- Relevant product or fashion imagery
- A clear Contact Us call to action

Keep the content professional, authentic, and concise.

## 6. COLLECTIONS / PRODUCTS PAGE

Create a professional product-category browsing experience.

Requirements:

- Separate sections or pages for relevant product categories.
- High-quality product images with consistent presentation.
- Product names and descriptions taken from verified available information.
- A clean, responsive image grid.
- Suitable image proportions and consistent spacing.
- Category navigation and working internal links.
- A clear Shop Online button linking to https://myzesh.in/.

Do not fabricate prices, product specifications, sizes, colours, stock availability, or product claims.

If detailed product information is unavailable, present the products as a visual portfolio instead of inventing details.

## 7. PORTFOLIO / GALLERY PAGE

Create a premium visual portfolio for ZESH.

Include:
- Product photography
- Fashion campaign images
- Collection imagery
- Brand visuals
- Additional company photographs when available

Organise images into appropriate categories.

Use a responsive grid or editorial gallery layout with consistent image treatment. Support image enlargement or a lightbox if the platform allows it.

Create a dedicated asset structure so that images can be replaced and new photos can be added easily.

Do not reuse the same photograph repeatedly merely to fill empty spaces. If an image is unavailable, omit that gallery item until a real asset is supplied.

## 8. CONTACT US AND LEAD GENERATION

Create a professional business enquiry page for both customer enquiries and business leads.

### Enquiry form fields
- Full Name
- Company / Business Name (optional)
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Enquiry Type
- Message

Enquiry type options should include:
- General Enquiry
- Product Enquiry
- Retail Partnership
- Wholesale Enquiry
- Distribution Enquiry
- Bulk Order
- Other

Requirements:
- Required-field validation.
- Email and phone validation.
- Clear submission and error states.
- Spam protection where supported.
- A working form submission connected to an actual email, CRM, database, or form-processing service.
- A clear privacy notice and consent where appropriate.
- Never display a false success message if the submission fails.

If a backend or form service has not been configured, clearly indicate what needs to be connected before launch.

### Contact options
Include:
- Click-to-call phone link, once the correct number is verified.
- WhatsApp enquiry button using the verified WhatsApp number.
- Email enquiry link using the verified business email.
- Business address and operating hours taken from the existing website, after checking for conflicting or demo information.

For WhatsApp, create a pre-filled message suitable for business enquiries. Do not invent a phone number.

## 9. LOGO AND BRANDING

Use the existing ZESH logo as the starting point.

Refine the visual presentation of the logo if necessary, but preserve the recognisable brand name and identity.

Do not replace the brand logo with plain text unless no usable logo file is available. If the logo requires redesign, keep the new design consistent with the original brand recognition.

Use the same logo, typography, colour palette, buttons, and spacing system throughout the website.

## 10. IMAGE AND ASSET MANAGEMENT

Prepare an organised asset structure for future uploads, such as:

- /assets/logo/
- /assets/hero/
- /assets/products/pants/
- /assets/products/joggers/
- /assets/products/trousers/
- /assets/products/palazzos/
- /assets/products/leggings/
- /assets/products/track-pants/
- /assets/gallery/
- /assets/brand/

Adapt the folder structure to the capabilities of the selected platform.

Use actual ZESH product images and uploaded company assets wherever available. Keep images easy to replace without redesigning the entire website.

Do not insert generic sample images as if they were actual ZESH products. If temporary imagery is necessary during development, label it clearly as temporary and make replacement straightforward.

## 11. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Responsive and mobile-first layout.
- Fast page loading and optimised images.
- Semantic structure and accessible colour contrast.
- Descriptive image alt text.
- SEO-friendly URLs, titles, descriptions, and headings.
- Open Graph metadata for social sharing.
- Working navigation and internal links.
- Clear error, loading, and success states for interactive components.
- Basic analytics integration if supported and configured.
- Appropriate privacy and security practices.
- No broken links, dead buttons, or non-functional forms.

## 12. FINAL QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Before completing the website:

1. Verify all five pages and navigation links.
2. Test the layout on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
3. Check that all product categories display the correct content.
4. Verify that the Shop Online button opens https://myzesh.in/.
5. Test all contact links and enquiry form behaviour.
6. Remove unrelated demo content and placeholder business information.
7. Check image consistency, typography, spacing, and responsiveness.
8. Confirm that the design looks cohesive and premium across all pages.

## FINAL INSTRUCTION

Build the complete ZESH corporate website as a cohesive, production-oriented brand experience.

The primary goals are to strengthen brand credibility, showcase the actual product portfolio, direct retail customers to the existing online store, and generate genuine business enquiries from retailers, wholesalers, distributors, and bulk buyers.

Use existing verified business information wherever available. Do not fabricate missing details.

**Important:** If a ZESH website project already exists in the current builder, update that project rather than creating a duplicate. Preserve existing working functionality unless a change is specifically required.

Deliver a polished, fully responsive website with a premium fashion identity, functional navigation, organised product galleries, and a properly configured enquiry flow.
